Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short. Pista Nera does not ask for an account, email or phone number, uses no cookies and shows no ads today. It only collects what is needed to run the races and to see whether the game works well.

Who is responsible for the data

Pista Nera is a game by C-Y-F Gaming. Responsible: . Contact: .

What the game collects

  • An anonymous code. The first time you open it, the game creates a random code and saves it on your device. It is used to recognise the same player in leaderboards and points. It contains neither your real name nor phone data.
  • The name you choose and your character. Other players see the name (up to 16 characters) in races and leaderboards: do not use your full name. Language, jersey and character look stay on the device; if you use the "personal code" in the menu they are also saved on the server, so you can find them on another device.
  • Game results. Times, positions, points and prizes won, linked to the anonymous code and the chosen name.
  • Voice messages. Only when you press the microphone button: the message (8 seconds at most) is sent to the other players in the same room and is not stored on the server. The microphone never turns on by itself. Voice messages can be switched off in the menu.
  • Reports. If a voice message is reported, the server remembers who was reported only for that challenge.
  • Anonymous technical measurements. Once a day: that the game was opened, how long it took to load, whether on a phone or a computer, where you came from (challenge link, app, website) and the average smoothness of the picture.
  • Game errors. If the game stops working it sends the error message, the game version, the type of system and browser and the screen size. These are deleted after 30 days.

What it does not collect

Location, contacts, photos, email, phone number, advertising identifiers. We do not sell or share data with anyone.

On your device

The game saves in the browser (local storage, not cookies) the anonymous code, the name, the language, the character and your choices, for example voice messages on or off and muted players. It also keeps a copy of the pages so it can show a notice when you are offline. To delete everything, clear the site data in your browser or uninstall the app.

Who else handles data

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and the race server: like any internet service it sees the device's IP address in order to deliver the pages.
  • Google Fonts provides the text fonts: the browser requests them from Google, which sees the IP address.
  • PayPal, only on the website and only if you choose the link to support the game with a donation: the payment happens on PayPal, under its own rules. The Android app does not have this link.

Advertising

The game shows no ads today. If ads are turned on in the future, this page will be updated first and, where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent.

How long

Game errors: 30 days. Daily track leaderboards: about 13 months. Name, points, leaderboards and anonymous measurements are kept as long as the game needs them, or until you ask for them to be deleted.

Your rights

You can ask which data is linked to your code and have it corrected or deleted: write to the contact above and give your "personal code" (in the game menu). If you live in the European Union you can also contact the data protection authority of your country.

Age

Pista Nera is meant for people aged 16 or over.
